About

Important details.

Description

PulseRecipes helps you import supported recipes, organize your collection, adjust servings, rate meals and record meal history. Lifetime Premium adds more recipe capacity, iCloud sync and recipe-generated shopping lists. Import results depend on the source page; review imported ingredients and nutrition before use.

Privacy

Pulse Recipes is designed to work locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or share personal information. Premium users can enable iCloud sync to keep recipes available across devices.

Your recipes, ingredients, and meal data stay on your device. We do not use ad networks or external tracking tools.
