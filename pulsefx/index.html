About

Important details.

Description

PulseFX helps you calculate position size, organize trading accounts and set reminders. Pro adds detailed trade journaling, charts, capital movements and weekly R/R reports. PulseFX does not place trades.

Disclaimer

PulseFX is provided for educational and informational purposes only. It does not provide financial advice, investment recommendations, or guarantees of trading performance.

You remain fully responsible for your own trading decisions and risk management. Only trade with capital you can afford to lose.

Privacy

PulseFX is designed to work locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or share personal information such as your name, email address, or payment details.

Account data, trade settings, notes, and alerts remain on your device. We do not use ad networks or external tracking tools.
