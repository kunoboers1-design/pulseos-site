Features

What PulseSideQuest gives you.

72 Meaningful Sidequests

Built-in prompts across attention, creativity, connection, movement, and reflection — enough variety to stay fresh without ever feeling repetitive.

  • Five focus areas to draw from
  • A new sidequest whenever you want one
  • No streaks or pressure to keep up

Quest Queue

Save a sidequest for later instead of doing it — or losing it — on the spot. Build a short list of ideas to return to when the moment is right.

  • Save quests for later
  • Come back whenever you're ready
  • Keep your favorites close

Rex Radar & Rex Routes

Not sure what to do next? Rex Radar gives you one personal recommendation. Rex Routes string several sidequests together for a longer outing.

  • One-tap personal recommendation
  • Multi-quest routes for longer breaks
  • Built around how you've been going

Journey & Reflection

Every completed sidequest becomes part of your journey, with room for a short reflection, a field note, or a photo as proof.

  • Reflections, field notes & photo proof
  • Week in Motion overview
  • Export your journey data as JSON

Live Activities & Reminders

A gentle daily nudge from Rex, plus a Live Activity that keeps your current sidequest visible without opening the app.

  • Daily Rex reminders
  • Live Activities support
  • Nothing naggy or repetitive

Privacy Lock

Keep your journey private with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode, so your reflections stay yours alone.

  • Face ID and Touch ID support
  • Device passcode fallback
  • Locked automatically once enabled

Quest Studio

Design your own sidequests from scratch and start them whenever inspiration hits.

  • Unlimited custom sidequests
  • Save, edit, and start anytime
  • Included free, no unlock needed

Privacy

Your journey stays yours.

On your device

Your journey history, reflections, and field notes stay on your device. There's nothing to sync to a server you don't control.

No account needed

Start using PulseSideQuest right away — no sign-up, no email address, and no account required.

No tracking or ads

PulseSideQuest doesn't use ad networks or tracking tools, and it doesn't sell or share your data.

Privacy Lock

Add an extra layer of protection with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode, whenever you want it.
