Discogs API

Connected to 8 million+ releases

PulseVinyl uses the Discogs API to check vinyl and CD availability, look up barcodes, and estimate collection value. You can optionally connect your own Discogs account — this is used only for those lookups, not to sync or upload your PulseVinyl collection to Discogs.

Privacy by design

Your music taste is yours.

PulseVinyl has no ads, behavioural tracking or third-party analytics. We don't sell your data or use it for marketing. Your collection is stored on your device and synced through your private iCloud account.

No ads or tracking

PulseVinyl contains no advertising networks or behavioural tracking tools.

Private collection

Owned and wanted records, notes and collection details are stored locally and synced through your private iCloud database.

Purpose-only music access

Apple Music access is used only to read the album and artist information required for PulseVinyl's features.

Transparent lookups

Album titles, artist names, barcodes or release IDs may be sent directly to Discogs or MusicBrainz when needed to identify a record. These services process requests under their own privacy policies.

When identifying music, the microphone is active only during recognition. Audio is not stored by PulseVinyl. ShazamKit converts captured audio into a one-way acoustic signature and does not share the original audio with Apple.

Services PulseVinyl uses
Apple Music Music-library and catalogue access after explicit permission.
iCloud / CloudKit Private collection synchronisation between your own devices.
Discogs Release information, barcode matching, artwork and marketplace prices. Optional account connection tokens are stored securely in the iPhone Keychain.
MusicBrainz & Cover Art Archive Fallback barcode metadata and artwork.
ShazamKit Music recognition without storing microphone recordings.
Frankfurter Currency conversion rates; no collection contents are included.

Read more in the privacy policies of Apple, Discogs, and MusicBrainz. PulseOS doesn't run its own user-profile or analytics database, and we can't browse your music library or collection — see the full Privacy Policy for details.

About

About

About PulseVinyl.

Description

PulseVinyl is a vinyl and CD collection tracker for Apple Music users. It scans your entire Apple Music library, checks every album for vinyl and CD availability via Discogs, and lets you track what you own, what you want, and what your collection is worth — all from your iPhone.

Privacy

PulseVinyl connects to Apple Music using on-device permissions, and your collection is stored on your device and synced through your private iCloud account — PulseOS doesn't store it on our servers. See Privacy by design above for the full picture, including what Discogs, MusicBrainz and ShazamKit are used for.
