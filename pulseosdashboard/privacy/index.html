PulseOS Dashboard

Privacy Policy.

What the private app reads, why it needs it, and how you stay in control.

Effective date: November 13, 2026

1. Who is responsible

PulseOS Dashboard is operated by Kuno Boers under the PulseOS name, in the Netherlands. Contact support@pulseos.eu for privacy questions or requests. This policy covers PulseOS Dashboard and these informational pages, rather than the other PulseOS apps.

The app is currently a private development tool for its operator’s own planning and authorized social accounts, with no public sign-up.

2. Information used by Dashboard

  • Your own records: tasks, ideas, notes, reflections, content plans, settings and imported reports.
  • Selected calendars and reminders: read through Apple’s EventKit after permission. Dashboard stores selection identifiers and its own planning intentions, rather than a persistent copy of the source calendar or reminders.
  • TikTok account information: the account’s app-specific identifier, display name and follower count.
  • Public TikTok video information: identifiers, titles, sharing links, creation times and available view, like, comment and share counts. The app reads counts, not the text of viewers’ comments.
  • Connection credentials: an authorized access token used to call the TikTok API. Dashboard does not ask for or store your TikTok password.

The TikTok scopes are user.info.basic, user.info.stats and video.list. The app uses these for account identification and performance reporting. It does not request posting permissions or access to private messages.

3. Purpose and authorization

Data is used to provide planning views, connect the correct account and create historical performance reports. TikTok access is optional and requires the account owner’s authorization. Data is not sold or used for advertising profiles.

Where data-protection law applies, optional account access is based on your authorization and consent where required; requested app functionality and support are processed to provide that service; limited website security processing serves the legitimate interest of maintaining a reliable website.

4. Storage and recipients

Dashboard records and imported reports are stored in local files on your Mac. Tokens are stored in the local macOS Keychain and are not included in Dashboard’s exports or companion synchronization. Optional export or folder synchronization, including an iCloud Drive folder you select, creates copies in that destination. Anyone with access to those files or devices may be able to read the copied records.

The TikTok integration sends authenticated requests directly to TikTok’s official API. TikTok processes those requests under its own Privacy Policy. Optional iCloud storage is processed by Apple under its Privacy Policy. Dashboard does not upload TikTok reports to this public website.

The website is hosted through Cloudflare. Hosting and network providers may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, browser information and requested URLs, for delivery and security. These three pages do not add analytics scripts or advertising cookies. Provider processing may involve countries outside your own; their applicable policies describe locations and safeguards.

5. Retention, disconnecting and deletion

Local records and report history remain until you delete them; no fixed automatic deletion period is currently implemented. Backups and exported or synchronized copies may outlast the local record and must be removed separately.

Use “Verwijder lokale koppeling” in the app’s connection settings to remove its saved TikTok token. This does not delete imported history or revoke the permission at TikTok. Revoke the app’s access separately in your TikTok account’s app-permission settings.

For assistance removing imported reports, local records or copies, email support@pulseos.eu. Include the app name and the records concerned, never your password or token. The operator cannot remotely erase files held only on your devices. Support correspondence is retained for as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations.

6. Security and your rights

The app uses HTTPS for TikTok requests and the operating system’s Keychain for credentials. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your devices, backups and shared folders.

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, deletion or a copy of personal data, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact the address above. You may also raise a complaint with your local data-protection authority, including the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

7. Changes

We update this page when the app’s data handling changes and show the effective date above. A future public release or integrated sign-in flow may require an updated policy and additional authorization.
